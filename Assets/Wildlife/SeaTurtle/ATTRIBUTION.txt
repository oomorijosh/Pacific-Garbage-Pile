Project preparation:
The source GLB is retained untouched. The official Unity glTFast 6.19.0
importer preserves the model's existing rig, skin weights, and "Swim Cycle"
animation. A wrapper centers, normalizes, and orients the imported model for
the exhibit. Prepared/SeaTurtle.prefab is the reusable prepared prefab, and
Prepared/SeaTurtle.controller is its looping Generic Animator controller.

Usage:
Run Tools > Pacific Exhibit > Add or Refresh Sea Turtle to refresh the exhibit
turtle and its prepared prefab. Enter Play mode to see the swimming animation.
Open Prepared/SeaTurtle.controller in the Animator window to inspect the
Swim Cycle clip and adjust the state's Speed.

Changes made for this project:
Converted the original static GLB to OBJ/MTL for Unity import, preserving mesh
positions, UVs, normals, triangle topology, material names, and base colors.
The original has four flat-color materials; it contains no image textures,
skeleton, or animations. Exhibit materials and placement may be adapted.
The Unity authoring helper centers and normalizes the model, recreates its
four source colors as matte URP materials, and places it in the exhibit's
upper water band. Specular highlights, environment reflections, and emission
are disabled on the turtle's materials.

Archived files:

SeaTurtle.obj and SeaTurtle.mtl - local static conversion
