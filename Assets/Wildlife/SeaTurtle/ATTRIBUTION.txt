Changes made for this project:
Converted the original static GLB to OBJ/MTL for Unity import, preserving mesh
positions, UVs, normals, triangle topology, material names, and base colors.
The original has four flat-color materials; it contains no image textures,
skeleton, or animations. Exhibit materials and placement may be adapted.
The Unity authoring helper centers and normalizes the model, recreates its
four source colors as matte URP materials, and places it in the exhibit's
upper water band. Specular highlights, environment reflections, and emission
are disabled on the turtle's materials.
