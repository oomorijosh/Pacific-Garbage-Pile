EXHIBIT AUDIO

Undertow.mp3 and calm wave.mp3 each play as continuous loops. Both start from
silence; music fades to a quiet steady 0.16 volume. Waves fade toward 0.4 at
the top of the water and toward exact silence throughout the lowest Micro
band, with 0.65-second smoothing so travel between layers does not jump.

The lowest 8% of the water is silent (Silent Bottom Fraction = 0.08).
Above it, the wave target follows SmoothStep from zero at the Micro band's
upper boundary to Waves Max Volume at the surface.
It follows CC_HEAD's actual world position converted into the continuous
GPGPWaterCompartment's local coordinates. It does not follow the selected
debris category, and it does not use the headset room's tracking camera.
The water compartment's position, rotation, scale and size are respected.
At the bottom, an inaudible smoothing tail settles to exact zero; playback
continues underneath so rising again does not restart the recording.

Both sources use stereo 2D playback. Wave volume conveys vertical closeness
to the surface, while retaining the recording's stereo sound regardless of
the direction the viewer faces. The waves source is positioned at the water
surface for inspection. This mix is an exhibit atmosphere rather than a
claim about how actual ocean sounds attenuate with depth.

The audio root is separate from the UI. X/B travel, category changes, and
UI visibility do not restart or stop either track. No listener, camera,
navigation binding, or global audio setting is created or changed.
Disabling the audio component pauses its sources. Deactivating the entire
audio root can stop them in Unity; reactivating restores their last observed
playheads and fades the same loops back in rather than starting at zero.

Setup: Tools > Pacific Exhibit > Add or Update Exhibit Audio. The command
adds one independent GPGP Exhibit Audio root, or updates its two owned
sources and references. Existing volume and smoothing settings are kept.
The operation supports Undo. Save the scene after setup.

Tune Music Volume, Waves Max Volume, Silent Bottom Fraction, and Mix Smooth
Seconds on the audio root after listening on the final exhibit speakers.
Unity's native looping is enabled. Listen for an audible seam in the
supplied files and adjust the recording if needed.
