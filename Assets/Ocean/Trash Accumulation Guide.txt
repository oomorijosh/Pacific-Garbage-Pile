TRASH ACCUMULATION VISUALIZATION

Open Assets/Scenes/Trash_Accumulation.unity and press Play.
The ocean and debris are generated at runtime; the scene uses a normal monitor camera.

Select Trash Accumulation in the Hierarchy to configure:
- Total Amount: the estimated total from your dataset (78,909 metric tonnes from Lebreton et al., 2018; historical 2015-2016 surveys).
- Unit: items, kg, tonnes, etc. Use the same unit as your source.
- Dataset Label: location and time period, including whether this is a stock or a flow.
- Source: citation or URL for the data.
- Target Count: number of visual blocks, from 1 to 1000 (default 400).
- Items Per Second: visual block reveal speed, not an environmental dumping rate.

Each block represents Total Amount / Target Count in the specified unit.
Blocks fall and settle on the wave surface. The counter increases on landing.
Blocks do not form a rigid stack or collide: they illustrate a growing surface patch.
Their colors and sizes are illustrative, not category or mass measurements.
The display identifies the source and the amount represented by each block.
Pause/Resume, Restart and reveal-speed controls are available during Play.
Set dataset fields before Play. After changing Target Count, click Restart.

The default is the historical GPGP mass estimate, not a current census.
See Assets/Debris/Great Pacific Garbage Patch Data.txt for uncertainty and sources.
Setting Total Amount to 0 displays Waiting for dataset and prevents spawning.
