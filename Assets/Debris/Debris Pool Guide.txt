DEBRIS MODEL POOLS

Open Assets/Scenes/Plastic_Type.unity and select Cube (1), which has the
GPGP Layered Cube Visualization component. Stop Play mode before rebuilding.

LOAD THE UPDATED CLUTTER AND TURTLE INTO AN OPEN SCENE

Click Apply Updated Debris + Turtle in the component Inspector, or use
Tools > Pacific Exhibit > Apply Updated Debris + Turtle. This refreshes the
category pools and clutter, adds the turtle, and balances existing presentation
lighting. Your other scene objects stay in place. Save the scene afterward.
The action supports Undo and does not automatically save or reopen a scene.

ADD MORE ITEMS

1. Import your model and textures into the matching Assets/Debris folder:
   Microplastic, Mesoplastic, Macroplastic, or Megaplastic.
2. For a textured model, make a Universal Render Pipeline/Lit material,
   assign its textures, apply it to the model, and save the model as a prefab
   in that category folder. Keep any multi-part materials in their own slots.
3. Click Refresh Debris Pools + Rebuild. It finds new models and prefabs,
   keeps manually added pool entries, and removes duplicate references.
4. Save the scene. The placed pieces remain editable in the Hierarchy.

MANUALLY EDIT A POOL

Drag models or prefabs into the named Micro Models, Meso Models, Macro Models,
or Mega Models lists. Click Rebuild From Current Pools to apply exactly those
lists. Use this button after removing entries you no longer want; folder refresh
adds available folder models again. Save the scene after rebuilding.

Every assigned model appears once before random repetitions when density is
above zero. Expanding a pool beyond its usual piece count adds enough pieces
to show every model. Band heights continue to encode the existing mass shares.

TEXTURES

The supplied texture sets are connected through reusable prefabs in
Assets/Debris/Prepared and materials in Assets/Debris/Materials. Folder refresh
updates these prepared assets. Save your own edited prefabs in the category
folders so they remain separate from the prepared texture library.

Base color belongs in Base Map. Normal textures must import as Normal Map.
AO, metallic and roughness textures contain linear data; disable sRGB for them.
URP Lit uses metallic in the red channel and smoothness in alpha, where
smoothness is 1 minus roughness. The supplied packs are converted automatically.
The lifebuoy ring and rope use separate materials; the bottle uses its supplied
transparency. Untextured models without compatible authored materials use the
exhibit palette. Compatible imported materials retain their colors and textures
through shared matte copies in the generated Prepared and Materials folders.

The importer preserves compatible URP materials on generated pieces. Water,
gallery floor, lighting and cameras are preserved when rebuilding debris.

CLUTTER, SIZES AND MOVEMENT

Select Four mass-share debris bands under the baked water cube to adjust the
random seed, cluster strength/count, and upward mixing fractions. Rebuild From
Current Pools applies those settings. Larger props stay in their own category;
Meso props also appear in Macro and Mega, and Macro props also appear in Mega.
These are added copies, so mixing does not empty the source category.

Micro stays static. The other categories gently drift and tumble inside their
assigned section. Random rotations and movement origins persist in the scene.
Debris sizes are exaggerated for visibility in the exhibit; the band height
shares continue to convey the existing data rather than actual ocean depth.

Prepared debris materials have emission, specular highlights and environment
reflections disabled to keep them from glowing under the exhibit lighting.

SEA TURTLE

Tools > Pacific Exhibit > Add or Refresh Sea Turtle adds the credited turtle
asset beside the debris. It has its own Sea Turtle branch and survives debris
rebuilds. Its source and redistribution credits are in
Assets/Wildlife/SeaTurtle/ATTRIBUTION.txt.
